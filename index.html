Who We Are

We are the graduate students, postdocs, and faculty of all ranks — including non-tenure-track and contingent faculty — who teach and do research at Rice University. Together we are the Rice University chapter of the American Association of University Professors (AAUP), a national union and membership association advancing academic freedom, shared governance, and the economic security of the profession. Join

Membership is open to the full academic community at Rice: graduate students, postdocs, and faculty of all ranks, including non-tenure-track and contingent faculty. Membership is confidential, and dues are scaled by income. Members receive legal support, information sharing, and community from the local to the national level. For Rice, please join through the Texas AAUP-AFT platform.
